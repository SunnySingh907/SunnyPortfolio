About

I'm Sunny, a developer who builds things that live between design and code — sites that respond to movement, light, and touch instead of sitting still.

My work blends clean interface design with real-time 3D and motion, turning ordinary pages into small interactive worlds. I care about the details most people scroll past: the easing curve, the depth of a shadow, the weight of a typeface.

Outside of client work, I experiment with generative art, shaders, and anything that makes a browser feel less like a document and more like a place.
